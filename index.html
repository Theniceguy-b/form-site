Custom covers and wraps for personal robots.

Clip-on 3D pieces and precision wraps for self-expression. Designed to stay clear of joints and sensors.

About the products

Our 3D covers are lightweight, clip-on pieces made from durable matte plastic. They sit outside the robot’s moving parts so they don’t interfere with motion or sensors. Each set is shaped for a specific look — soft and playful, clean and minimal, or more ornate.

Wraps are precision-cut sticker kits. Solid colors for a clean finish, or patterns for more graphic looks. They go on and come off without leaving residue.

Materials are chosen for low weight and easy cleaning. The covers are designed to be swapped, so the same robot can change appearance without tools.

FORM is a small studio focused on aftermarket customization for personal humanoid robots. We design the pieces, then work with low-volume manufacturers for wrap cutting and 3D printing.
